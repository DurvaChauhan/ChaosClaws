## 🪞 The story behind this one

This isn't just a landing page — it's a first. The first time a design lived only in my head, then in a sketchbook, then in code, and then in front of a real client who said *yes, this is it.*

ChaosClaws is a nail studio built on a simple idea: your hands are an outfit, not an afterthought. Every animation, every color, every line of copy on this page was built to match that energy — a little dramatic, a little handmade, entirely intentional.

<br>

## ✨ The vibe

| | |
|---|---|
| 🎀 **Handmade, not templated** | Every pixel here was designed and coded from a blank page — no theme, no template, no shortcuts. |
| 🖤 **A mood, not just a menu** | Wine, cream, and tan tones. Floating hearts. A ticker that never stops moving. It's a manicure you can scroll through. |
| ✍️ **Voice-first copy** | The writing does as much work as the design — it's meant to sound like the studio talking directly to you. |
| 💫 **Built to feel alive** | Subtle motion throughout — nothing static, nothing stiff — because nail art isn't either. |

<br>

## 🎨 Inside the design

The palette wasn't picked at random — deep wine and warm tan, the same colors you'd find in a jewelry box, not a tech product. A few details worth noticing if you slow down and scroll:

- **The floating hearts** drifting in the background — barely-there, so they read as texture, not decoration
- **The ticker up top** that never actually stops moving, the same restlessness as scrolling through a camera roll of nail inspo
- **The glitter set** in "The Drops" — the one card that gets to be a little louder than the rest
- **The loyalty stamp** — a little wink to regulars, styled like something you'd find stamped on real packaging

None of it is accidental. A studio like this lives or dies by whether it *feels* like the work it's selling — so the site had to feel handmade too.
